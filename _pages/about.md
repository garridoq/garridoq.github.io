I'm Quentin Garrido, a Member of Technical Staff at <a href="https://amilabs.xyz/">AMI Labs</a> in Paris, working broadly on **Self-Supervised Learning** and **World Models**.

My research focuses on learning representations and world models from natural data such as raw videos, with work spanning video self-supervised learning ([V-JEPA](https://openreview.net/forum?id=QaCCuDfBk2), [V-JEPA 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985)), latent action world models, and the emergence of physics understanding in video models.
Earlier in my career I worked on the foundations of self-supervised learning and its applications to computer vision and natural sciences.

Before joining AMI Labs, I was a Research Scientist at <a href="https://ai.meta.com/">FAIR at Meta</a>.
I obtained my PhD in computer science from <a href="https://ai.meta.com/">FAIR at Meta</a> and <a href="https://www.univ-gustave-eiffel.fr/en/">Université Gustave Eiffel</a> in 2025, co-advised by <a href="https://yann.lecun.com/">Yann LeCun</a> and <a href="https://www.laurentnajman.org/">Laurent Najman</a>.
I also hold a master's degree from the [Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage (MVA)](https://www.master-mva.com/) program at [École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/),
as well as an engineering degree in computer science from [ESIEE Paris](https://www.esiee.fr/).
